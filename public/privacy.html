Your information.
Handled with care.

This privacy policy explains what Netdin collects through this website, why we collect it, and the choices available to you.

What we collect

When you send a project enquiry, we collect your name, email address, company if provided, selected services, budget preference, timeline, project message, and consent to be contacted.

When you use the Netdin chatbot, your messages and any details you choose to share may be processed by the chatbot service so it can respond and help us understand your request. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, confidential credentials, or sensitive personal information through the chatbot or enquiry form.

Hosting and security systems may also process technical information such as browser, device, request, and error data.

How we use your information

  • To review and respond to project enquiries.
  • To send an acknowledgement when an enquiry is received.
  • To answer general questions through the chatbot.
  • To improve the website, our services, and our response process.
  • To protect the website and services from misuse.

We do not sell enquiry or chatbot information. We do not add you to a marketing list without separate permission.

Service providers

Netdin uses service providers to operate this website and handle enquiries. These may include:

  • Appwrite for website hosting and secure enquiry storage.
  • Zoho Mail for the Netdin business inbox.
  • Resend for automated email delivery.
  • Botpress for chatbot operation and conversation processing.

These providers may process information in countries other than your own. Each provider handles information under its own terms and privacy documentation.

Storage and retention

Project enquiries are stored in Appwrite with access restricted to the Netdin team. Website visitors can submit an enquiry but cannot read, change, or delete stored enquiries.

We keep enquiry information only for as long as it is needed for the conversation, related services, and applicable legal or business obligations. Chatbot retention may also depend on the Botpress configuration and service terms.

Chatbot limitations

The chatbot provides general information and may make mistakes. It is not a substitute for a human reply, professional advice, or a confirmed project proposal. Questions about scope, price, commitments, or sensitive matters are reviewed by a Netdin team member.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your enquiry information by emailing hello@netdin.com. Please include enough information for us to identify the conversation.

You can also stop using the chatbot or ask us not to use your information for follow-up communication.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy when the website, services, or data practices change. The date at the top shows when the current version was last updated.

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@netdin.com.
